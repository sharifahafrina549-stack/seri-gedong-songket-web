About Seri Gedong Songket

Seri Gedong Songket is committed to preserving Sarawak's traditional weaving heritage by producing premium handcrafted Songket and related products.

Meet the Founder

Ramtiniwaiti binti Ramlee founded Seri Gedong Songket in Kampung Gedong, Simunjan, Sarawak, taking root in the village from 2015 before being formally registered as Seri Gedong Enterprise in March 2016.

Under her leadership, the enterprise grew from a village-based weaving initiative into a brand - Maskota - trademarked with MYIPO and recognized on international platforms including the Mobile World Congress in Barcelona and the Malaysia-China Summit.

Our Weaving Process

From raw thread to finished songket - every piece is handwoven

1

Thread Dyeing

Natural dyes are used according to the selected songket design and colour requirements.

2

Thread Winding

The threads are unwound and arranged onto bobbins to prepare them for the patterning process.

3

Thread Arrangement

Thread are arranged one by one to determine the fabric's length and width while organising the colour sequence according to the customer's requirements.

4

Warp Winding

The arranged threads are measured according to the required fabric width and wound onto the warp beam.

5

Reeding

Each thread is inserted individually through the reed or heddle eyes to prepare for weaving.

6

Drafting

The heddle threads are connected to the warp threads through the lease rods, separating them into upper and lower warp threads for weaving.

7

Warp Joining

The new warp threads are tied to the existing warp threads, replacing the need to repeat the reading and drafting processes.

8

Warp Tensioning

The warp threads are stretched tightly onto the traditional weaving loom to ensure proper tension before weaving begins.

9

Pattern Weaving

Decorative motifs are created on the warp threads based on graph paper designs and the selected traditional pattern.

10

Weaving

The final stage involves passing the weft thread between the upper and lower warp threads on the loom until a complete piece of songket fabric is produced.

Company History

Seri Gedong Songket was founded by Ramtiniwaiti binti Ramlee in Kampung Gedong, Simunjan, Sarawak, taking root in the village from 2015 before being formally registered as Seri Gedong Enterprise in March 2016.

Maskota is the company's official product brand, holding a trademark registration with the Intellectual Property Corporation of Malaysia (MYIPO), built to carry Sarawak songket into international markets.

Beyond weaving fabric, Seri Gedong supplies weaving tools and raw materials, trains young weavers from the community, and has represented Sarawak's songket heritage at the Mobile World Congress in Barcelona and the Malaysia-China Summit, with products reaching the Canada and China.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about ordering, sizing, delivery and care

Do you accept custom orders?

Yes, we accept custom orders for colours, patterns, and sizing. Please contact us via WhatsApp or email with your requirements, and we'll confirm availability, pricing, and timeline.

How do I know what size to order?

Sampin, Sarong, and Selendang come in standard adult sizing. For a custom fit, please provide your measurements when ordering, or contact us for a sizing guide.

How long does delivery take?

Local (Sarawak/Malaysia) orders typically arrive within 3-7 working days.

How do I care for my songket?

Songket should be dry-cleaned only to preserve the gold/silver threads. Store folded in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight, ideally wrapped in breathable cloth rather than plastic.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfer and cash on delivery for local orders. See our Business Information section for full details.
